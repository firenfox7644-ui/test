Over the last few years, IndiGo has leveraged the Adobe Martech stack to support digital analytics, campaign management and personalization across web and mobile channels. This ecosystem has helped grow D2C share of wallet from single digits to nearly 19% of the total network. While the platform has enabled key business capabilities, customer expectations, digital channels and the Martech landscape have evolved significantly.
As IndiGo enters its next phase of digital growth through Project Phoenix, direct-channel acceleration, loyalty expansion, customer lifetime value optimization and AI-led engagement, Martech is becoming a strategic business enabler. Its role now extends beyond campaign execution and reporting to driving customer acquisition, conversion, personalization, retention and ancillary revenue growth.
The Adobe contract was renewed in early 2026 for a one-year period, with the understanding that IndiGo would use this time to review the broader Martech landscape, assess evolving business requirements and define its long-term Martech strategy.
Accordingly, the Web & Mobile team initiated three RFPs covering Analytics, Personalization and Martech/Campaign Management. The intent was not merely to evaluate a technology replacement but to assess the market and determine the best path forward for IndiGo.
The RFPs were designed to:
•	Align Martech capabilities with IndiGo's future digital growth ambitions.
•	Evaluate advancements in analytics, customer data, campaign orchestration and personalization.
•	Assess emerging AI and GenAI-driven marketing capabilities.
•	Benchmark the incumbent platform and roadmap against market alternatives on capability, scalability, innovation and total cost of ownership.
•	Simplify customer engagement architecture and reduce operational complexity.
•	Improve speed to market for personalization and campaign execution.
•	Ensure technology investments remain outcome-driven and deliver measurable business value.
Key Objectives & Outcomes
1. Market & Technology Evaluation
The RFPs enabled IndiGo to evaluate established and emerging solutions across Analytics, Personalization and Martech against clearly defined business and technology requirements, helping assess capability fit, scalability and product maturity.
2. Single-Stack vs Best-of-Breed Assessment
The exercise evaluated the trade-offs between a unified platform approach and specialized best-of-breed solutions, balancing integration simplicity and governance against flexibility and innovation.
3. Architecture & Integration Assessment
The RFPs provided insight into integration requirements, data architecture, interoperability, implementation complexity and long-term operating considerations across different solution approaches.
4. Future Scalability & Roadmap
The evaluation assessed each platform's ability to support future growth, evolving use cases, AI adoption, personalization maturity and data activation needs.
5. Commercial Benchmarking
The exercise established an independent benchmark for commercial competitiveness, total cost of ownership and value realization across incumbent and participating vendors.
The process has generated valuable market intelligence, capability benchmarks and insights into the strengths and limitations of different approaches. These findings will be instrumental in defining the optimal Martech strategy and technology ecosystem for IndiGo's future growth ambitions.
Expected Outcomes
The RFP will help IndiGo:
•	Define a future-ready Martech strategy.
•	Establish the target operating model for Analytics, Campaign Management and Personalization.
•	Evaluate build, buy and partner options.
•	Identify opportunities for platform consolidation and optimization.
•	Select the technology ecosystem best aligned with long-term business objectives.
•	Maximize ROI on Martech investments while supporting accelerated digital growth and customer experience goals.
Conclusion
As IndiGo enters its next phase of digital growth amidst evolving customer expectations, increasing regulatory focus and rapid advances in AI-driven engagement, it is important to reassess whether the current Martech ecosystem remains the optimal fit for future business objectives.
The RFP exercise has provided an objective assessment of the available technology landscape, including vendor capabilities, architectural options, scalability considerations and commercial benchmarks. The learnings from this exercise will form a key input into IndiGo's long-term Martech strategy and help determine whether the organization continues with an integrated platform approach or adopts a best-of-breed architecture that maximizes innovation, agility, business outcomes and return on investment.
