At a very high level, below would be the key objectives:

The objective of this Request for Proposal (RFP) is to identify and onboard a strategic technology partner capable of providing an Enterprise Digital Analytics Platform that enables IndiGo to measure, analyse, and optimize customer interactions across all digital channels.

The proposed solution should support IndiGo's current and future digital transformation initiatives and provide a scalable, secure, and data-driven foundation for business decision making.

The key objectives of this engagement are:

1. Enterprise Digital Analytics
Provide a unified analytics platform capable of collecting, processing, and reporting customer interaction data across all IndiGo digital touchpoints, including websites, mobile applications, conversational channels, and future digital properties.

2. Customer Journey Visibility
Enable end-to-end visibility into customer journeys across multiple channels and devices to understand user behaviour, identify friction points, and optimize conversion opportunities.

3. Product and Business Analytics
Provide actionable insights into product usage, customer engagement, ancillary purchases, booking behaviour, and operational performance through configurable dashboards and advanced reporting capabilities.

4. Marketing and Campaign Measurement
Enable accurate measurement of marketing campaigns, customer acquisition channels, attribution models, and return on investment for digital marketing initiatives.

5. Data Governance and Compliance
Provide enterprise-grade governance controls to ensure data quality, privacy, regulatory compliance, auditability, and secure management of customer information.

6. Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Analytics
Leverage AI and machine learning capabilities to support predictive analytics, customer segmentation, anomaly detection, churn prediction, and intelligent business recommendations.

7. Scalability and Future Readiness
Provide a platform capable of supporting future business growth, increased transaction volumes, additional digital channels, and emerging technologies without significant architectural changes.

The selected solution should minimize operational overhead, maximize business value realization, and support IndiGo's long-term digital strategy.

Solution capability will be evaluated for fitment of purpose. We may choose to discontinue or defer the project, implement any one of the functions, or implement multiple, depending upon the outcome of product evaluation and commercial considerations.

The proposed solution should support, but not be limited to, the following capability areas:

1. Enterprise Data Collection & Digital Instrumentation
Provide a comprehensive analytics data collection framework capable of capturing customer interactions consistently across all IndiGo digital channels.
The solution should support collection of behavioural, transactional, operational, technical, and engagement events from multiple sources while ensuring high data quality, low latency, and governance.

2. Event Taxonomy & Data Governance
Provide an enterprise event governance framework enabling standardized event naming conventions, metadata management, version control, validation rules, event quality monitoring, and lifecycle management.
The platform should ensure consistency of analytics implementation across multiple digital products and teams.

3. Customer Identity Resolution & Unified Customer Profiles
Provide capabilities to identify, merge, and manage customer identities across anonymous, authenticated, loyalty, and partner channels.
The solution should create a unified customer profile enabling complete visibility of customer behaviour throughout the digital journey.

4. Customer Journey Analytics
Provide end-to-end visibility into customer journeys across all digital touchpoints.
The solution should enable visualization, measurement, optimization, and analysis of customer journeys across Web, Mobile Applications, APIs, Loyalty Platforms, Contact Centre interactions, Marketing Platforms, and future digital properties.

5. Product Analytics
Provide enterprise-grade product analytics enabling product managers and business teams to measure customer engagement, feature adoption, user behaviour, digital funnel performance, feature effectiveness, and overall product health.
6. Booking Funnel Analytics & Conversion Optimization
Provide specialized analytics for airline booking funnels enabling detailed analysis of customer progression across Search, Flight Selection, Passenger Details, Ancillary Selection, Payment, Confirmation, Check-in, and post-booking servicing journeys.
The solution should identify friction points, abandonment patterns, and optimization opportunities.

7. Marketing Analytics & Attribution
Provide comprehensive marketing analytics capabilities supporting campaign performance measurement, acquisition analysis, attribution modelling, channel effectiveness, customer acquisition cost, return on investment, and customer lifetime value.

8. Audience Management & Customer Segmentation
Provide enterprise audience management capabilities allowing business users to create dynamic customer segments using behavioural, transactional, demographic, loyalty, operational, contextual, and predictive attributes.
The solution should enable activation of audiences across enterprise marketing and personalization platforms.

9. AI-Powered Analytics & Predictive Intelligence
Provide Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning capabilities enabling predictive analytics, anomaly detection, customer propensity modelling, churn prediction, intelligent recommendations, automated insights, forecasting, and business optimization.
The platform should leverage both historical and real-time customer data to generate actionable recommendations.

10. Digital Experience Analytics
Provide advanced Digital Experience Analytics capabilities enabling organizations to understand customer interaction quality beyond traditional reporting.
Capabilities should include session replay, customer struggle detection, rage clicks, dead clicks, excessive scrolling, JavaScript errors, performance bottlenecks, usability analytics, and digital experience scoring.

11. Heatmaps & User Interaction Analytics
Provide visual interaction analytics enabling business users to understand customer engagement through click heatmaps, scroll depth analysis, attention maps, form analytics, interaction density, and content engagement visualization.

12. Reporting, Dashboards & Business Intelligence
Provide enterprise reporting capabilities supporting executive dashboards, operational dashboards, product analytics dashboards, marketing dashboards, airline operational reporting, custom dashboards, and self-service analytics.
The platform should support interactive reporting, drill-down capabilities, scheduled reports, real-time dashboards, and enterprise KPI monitoring.

13. Self-Service Analytics & Business User Enablement
Provide intuitive no-code or low-code capabilities enabling business users, analysts, marketing teams, and product managers to independently create reports, dashboards, customer segments, calculated metrics, alerts, and business insights without engineering dependency.

14. Enterprise Data Quality & Governance
Provide comprehensive governance capabilities ensuring data accuracy, completeness, consistency, auditability, lineage, metadata management, access controls, regulatory compliance, and enterprise data stewardship.
The vendor shall also provide periodic business reviews, adoption assessments, optimization recommendations, product roadmap updates, and strategic guidance to maximize the value realization from the Enterprise Digital Analytics Platform.
