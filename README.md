Web Personalization & Experimentation- Proof of Concept (“POC”)

Part B shall be valid from the Effective Date of the Agreement for a period of 6 months from the Go-Live of the Web Personalization & Experimentation- Proof of Concept.
The scope of the POC shall include:

1.	Implementation and Integration
The Service Provider shall configure and integrate the Web Personalization & Experimentation solution with IndiGo's web environment and such relevant systems, data sources, analytics platforms and interfaces as mutually agreed between the Parties for execution of the POC.

2.	Web Personalization
The solution shall enable creation and delivery of personalized web experiences, including modification and targeting of content, banners, messages, offers and other agreed web components based on customer attributes, behaviour, events, segments and contextual information.

3.	Experimentation
The solution shall support A/B, A/B/n and/or multivariate experimentation, as applicable, for agreed website components, customer journeys, content, messaging, offers and placements.

4.	Audience Segmentation and Targeting
The POC shall support creation and use of customer segments and targeting rules based on available user attributes, behavioural signals, events and other data made available by IndiGo.

5.	Campaign and Experience Management
The Service Provider shall enable authorized IndiGo users to configure, preview, test, activate, pause and manage agreed personalization and experimentation use cases through the Platform, with minimal engineering dependency to the extent supported by the solution.

6.	Measurement and Reporting
The Platform shall provide reporting and analytics for POC use cases, including applicable impressions, interactions, conversions, experiment variants, uplift/performance comparison and other mutually agreed success metrics required to assess the effectiveness of the POC.

7.	POC Use Cases and Success Criteria
The specific web journeys, experiments, personalization use cases, KPIs and acceptance/success criteria to be validated during the POC shall be mutually identified and documented by the Parties prior to or during implementation. Successful completion of the POC shall not, by itself, constitute an obligation on IndiGo to procure or continue the Services.

8.	Implementation Schedule
The implementation activities and Go-Live schedule shall be mutually agreed between the Parties. The implementation period prior to Go-Live shall not form part of the six (6) month POC period.

9.	POC Term
The POC shall commence from the successful Go-Live Date and shall remain valid for a period of six (6) months from such Go-Live Date.

10.	Commercials
The Web Personalization & Experimentation POC, including use of the Platform during the six (6) month POC, shall be provided to IndiGo free of cost, and no license, subscription or Platform usage charges shall be payable by IndiGo for such period, unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing.

11.	Support During POC
The Service Provider shall provide reasonable implementation, configuration, troubleshooting and technical support required for operation and evaluation of the agreed POC use cases throughout the POC period.

12. Post-POC Continuation
Upon expiry of the six (6) month POC period, IndiGo may, at its sole discretion, discontinue the POC or elect to continue the Services. Any continuation shall be subject to mutual written agreement and execution of an amendment to this Agreement capturing the commercial and other contractual. No automatic renewal or commercial commitment shall arise upon completion of the POC.

13.	No-Code / Low-Code Experience Management
The platform shall provide a no-code/low-code interface for designing and managing in-app experiences, customer engagement surfaces, and user journeys. It should enable dynamic modification of UI components, content, and workflows without requiring extensive development effort. The solution must support real-time preview, QA, and publishing capabilities to facilitate rapid testing and iteration. It should also provide one-click publishing with rollback functionality for seamless deployment and recovery. Additionally, the platform shall include role-based access controls, version management, and comprehensive audit logs to ensure governance, security, and traceability of all changes.

    14. Usability
The system should consume Figma/design files provided by IndiGo and adapt them into the app without engineering support. It should provide an intuitive UI for product managers and growth teams and enable easy creation and management of notifications (in-app and push) without engineering support. The system should also provide clear visibility into notification status, performance, and errors, along with simple workflows for QA, approvals, and publishing.

15. CDP-Driven Personalization
The solution should ingest user attributes, events, and predictions from IndiGo’s CDP. It should enable personalization of home screen modules, banners, and content using CDP data, and support conditional rendering of UI components based on CDP attributes.

16. Journey Orchestration & Automation
The solution should provide automated journey orchestration to deliver the right message at the right moment. It should include a visual journey builder with a drag-and-drop interface and support triggering journeys based on CDP segments and events from multiple sources. The solution should enable event-based triggers for reminders related to incomplete bookings, upcoming trips, price drops, and flight status updates. It should support branching logic based on user behavior, CDP attributes, and predictive scores, along with frequency capping and priority rules to avoid message overload. The solution should also orchestrate in-app messages and push notifications (where applicable).

17 Experimentation & Optimization
The solution should support A/B and multivariate testing for UI components, messaging, offers, and placements. It should provide A/B testing frameworks and experimentation tools to optimize UI/UX, messaging, pricing, placements, offers, ancillary pricing, bundles, and discount strategies. The solution should include automated significance calculation and winner selection, support holdout groups and long-term experiments, and provide experimentation insights and optimization recommendations.

18. Context-Aware UI Personalization
The solution should support personalization of UI themes based on the time of day. It should adapt home screen modules based on upcoming trip context and dynamically reorder app sections based on individual usage patterns. The solution should also provide contextual nudges when the cart is empty or a booking is incomplete.
